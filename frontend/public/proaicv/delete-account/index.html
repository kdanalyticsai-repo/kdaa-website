Delete Your Account

Last updated: 25 May 2026

You can request deletion of your ProAICV account and all associated data at any time. We will process your request within 30 days.

⚠️ This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your resume files, job matches, application history, and subscription data cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • All uploaded resume files (PDF)
  • Resume analysis and ATS scores
  • Saved jobs and application tracking records
  • AI coaching conversation history
  • Job match scores and preferences
  • Subscription and payment records (except where required by law)

What May Be Retained

  • Transaction records required for tax and legal compliance (up to 7 years as per Indian law)
  • Anonymised, aggregated usage data that cannot be linked back to you

Processing Time

We will confirm receipt of your request within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been fully deleted.
