Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

CVProAI ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by KDAA ANALYTICS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the CVProAI mobile application and related services.

By using CVProAI, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name, email address, and password when you register
  • Phone number (optional, if provided)
  • Profile information such as your career preferences
  • Google account details if you sign in with Google

Resume Data

  • Resume files (PDF) that you upload to the app
  • Parsed resume content including work experience, education, and skills
  • ATS scores and analysis results generated from your resume

Job & Application Data

  • Jobs you save, view, or mark as applied
  • Application tracking records you create within the app

Payment Information

  • Subscription plan and payment status
  • Payment transactions are processed by Razorpay — we do not store your card or UPI details

Device & Usage Data

  • Push notification tokens for sending you alerts
  • App usage data (features used, session activity)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Error logs and crash reports (via Sentry)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide job matching, ATS scoring, resume tailoring, and AI coaching features
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send push notifications about job matches, application updates, and subscription reminders (only if you opt in)
  • To improve the app, fix bugs, and monitor performance
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. AI Features

CVProAI uses OpenAI's API to power AI features including the Career Coach, resume tailoring, cover letter generation, and interview preparation. When you use these features, relevant portions of your resume and job details are sent to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy for more details.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Razorpay — payment processing (razorpay.com/privacy)
  • OpenAI — AI features (openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
  • Amazon Web Services (S3) — secure resume file storage
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Sentry — error monitoring and crash reporting
  • Google — Google Sign-In authentication

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted on Render (Oregon, USA) using PostgreSQL. Resume files are stored on Amazon S3 with private access controls.

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and JWT-based authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data and uploaded resumes will be permanently deleted within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via the app's Notification Settings
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where consent is the legal basis

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@kdaanalytics.com.

8. Children's Privacy

CVProAI is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via a push notification or email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of CVProAI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
